# Trust assumptions

- USDG and ordinary protocol/reward assets obey the documented standard non-rebasing ERC-20 transfer model; Fund alone
  enforces exact selected-asset payout deltas.
- The Mine and Resonance owners can exercise only their recorded narrow custom authority plus inherited ownership
  transfer and renunciation. External governance policy is not supplied or reviewed by this target.
- Mine Router validation proves reciprocal identities, not bytecode authenticity, pristine state, ownership, live
  Strategies, or economic safety. Those remain deployment-manifest gates.
- Permissionless routing may be delayed indefinitely. Delay cannot block Mine completion, claims, signal removal, or
  redemption.
- The target chain, USDG, Factory, Pair behavior, and governance executor require pinned external evidence before any
  release claim. A skipped fork is unavailable evidence, not a pass.
- Integer floors, direct-donation surplus, zero-signal elapsed revenue, omitted redemption assets, and delayed first
  Strategy inventory reaching zero price are documented economic consequences unless implementation diverges.
