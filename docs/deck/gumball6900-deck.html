The Index Fund That Chooses Itself

An onchain index fund that holds real assets — tokenized stocks, crypto, anything with an ERC-20 behind it. There is no manager. The people who hold the token decide what it buys, and any holder can burn their tokens to withdraw their share of the actual holdings, at any time, without asking anyone.

01

What it is

An onchain index fund that holds real, tokenized assets — and the people who hold the token pick what goes in it.

One pool of assets. One token, GBX, that represents a share of that pool. The fund earns revenue, spends it buying whatever its holders have pointed it at, and never sells. Burn your GBX whenever you like and your share of the holdings comes back to you.

It is a fund

A single treasury of real tokenized assets, with one token representing a claim on all of it.

The index picks itself

Constituents are chosen continuously by holders committing stake — not by a committee, a methodology, or a provider.

It only accumulates

Revenue converts into assets through open auctions. Nothing is ever sold, and there is no rebalancing.

You can always leave

Burn GBX and your share of the holdings arrives in the same transaction. No manager can refuse you, and there is nothing to apply for.

Think of it as an index whose constituents are decided by the conviction of the people who own it, and whose exit is arithmetic rather than a redemption desk.

03

How it works, end to end

One loop. Money in, aimed by holders, out as assets you can claim.

Every arrow is a contract call anyone can make, and there is no step where a person decides. Note where the money starts: miners pay USDG for slots, and that payment is what the fund ultimately spends. The rest of this deck walks each stage in order.

04

The token

GBX is a share of the fund, and it does exactly two things.

Signal with it

Commit it to a Strategy — a standing mandate to acquire one asset — and you direct where the protocol's revenue goes, and earn a share of everything that Strategy buys.

Burn it

Destroy your tokens and withdraw your proportional share of the treasury, in the assets you name, in the same transaction.

GBX cannot be created on a whim. It exists exactly two ways: a single 20,000,000 token allocation at deployment that goes straight into permanently locked market liquidity, and continuous issuance to miners after that. There is no team allocation, no presale, no treasury mint, and no second issuer — ever.

07

Resonance — running live

Signal decides the split. Move it, and the money follows.

Revenue from mining drains out over seven days and splits by whatever the signal weights are at that moment. Signalers keep moving their stake in whole lots, and the next dollar follows them.

week 1 Each Strategy sells its USDG in a falling-price auction, then 90% of the asset backs GBX.
08

Acquisition — running live

One stack rises, the other falls. When they meet, the trade happens.

A Strategy pools the USDG it was signalled and asks to be paid in QQQ itself, never in dollars. The lot keeps growing, so what it is worth rises. The ask falls in a straight line. The moment the ask drops to what the lot is worth, a trader takes it — USDG to them, QQQ back to the fund and the signalers. That is the whole price discovery; no oracle exists.

10

Why this does not exist yet

Onchain index funds keep failing in the same four places.

Plenty of people have tried to put an index onchain. The pattern of what breaks is consistent, and each break is a place where a human had to be trusted after all.

Who picks the constituents
A multisig or a foundation committee decides what is in, usually off-chain, usually opaque. That is a manager wearing a different hat.
Holders decide, continuously, by committing stake. Nobody can add or weight an asset by decree.
Getting your money out
Redemption is gated, batched, whitelisted to market makers, or simply absent — so the only real exit is selling on a thin secondary market.
Burn any amount at any moment and receive the underlying tokens. No queue, no gate, no counterparty.
Rebalancing
Expensive, MEV-exposed, and it needs a price feed to know what the target weights even are. It is where most designs quietly re-introduce an oracle.
There is no rebalancing. The fund never sells. Weights are an outcome of what holders chose, not a target anyone maintains.
Pricing
An oracle decides what everything is worth, so the whole structure inherits that oracle's failure modes and whoever controls it.
No oracle exists in the protocol. It lowers its asking price until a trader fills it. The auction is the price discovery.

Removing rebalancing is the trade that makes the rest possible. This is not a fund that tracks an index someone else publishes — it accumulates what its holders point it at, and never sells.

11

Why anyone would want in

Getting listed is not a badge. It is a standing order to buy your token.

A Strategy is a permanent mandate to acquire one asset. Once it exists and carries signal, a share of every dollar the protocol earns is spent buying that asset, week after week, with no regard for its price. For the asset on the other side of that, this is durable, mechanical, price-insensitive demand — and it does not stop unless holders move their signal away.

For a token that wants accumulation

Being an index constituent normally means paying an intermediary and satisfying a committee. Here it means attracting conviction — and the fund then buys you continuously, forever.

Signal what you want to be paid in

A tenth of everything a Strategy acquires goes to the people who signalled it, in that asset. Want to accumulate NVDA? Signal the NVDA Strategy and you are paid in NVDA.

And you can pay to be chosen

Anyone may stream extra rewards into a Strategy's pool to pull signal toward it. That is an open, priced market for the fund's buying power — not a listing fee to a gatekeeper.

Nothing here is a promise about anyone's token price. It is a description of where the protocol's revenue mechanically goes, and who decides.

12

The economic thesis

GBX is worth its share of the assets, plus the right to aim the next purchase.

Layer one

Redemption value

Burn your GBX and receive a pro-rata slice of everything in the Fund. This is arithmetic, always available, and it needs nobody's cooperation. If GBX ever trades below it, buying GBX to redeem it is a profitable trade — which is the mechanism that closes the gap.

Layer two

Signal power

Holding GBX is the only way to direct where future revenue is spent. To anyone who wants the fund accumulating their asset, that is worth paying for — and Bribes make exactly that demand explicit and priced. This layer has no redemption value at all; it is the market's price on control of future flows.

And

The Fund only ever accumulates

Nothing sells. There is no rebalancing, no fee skim, no manager taking profits off the table. The single outflow is a holder redeeming their own share, which removes their tokens from the denominator at the same time.

Stated as design intent, not as a forecast. Nothing guarantees GBX trades at or above its redemption value: that argument depends on the acquired assets being liquid enough to arbitrage, and on there being real demand for signal. Supply also grows continuously, so a holder who neither signals nor mines is diluted over time. This is the mechanism, not a promise about price.
