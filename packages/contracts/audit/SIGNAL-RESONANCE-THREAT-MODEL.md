# Signal and Resonance adversarial threat model

> **Pre-ADR-0047 historical assessment.** The attack matrix and outcomes below target the former exact-carry,
> queue/pause, deferred-liability, selected-batch, exact-delta, and weighted-split graph. ADR 0047 replaced those
> mechanics with scalar Synthetix scheduling, per-purchase Strategy splitting, direct Fund payment, and a Bribe-only
> Router. The table is preserved as historical evidence only; its test names and conclusions are not current.

Assessment date: 2026-08-16; authority, Bribe-cap, global-share, and reward-precision assumptions reconciled 2026-08-21
for ADRs 0034–0037. The
attacker may order transactions, split identities and payments, use callback-capable or malformed ERC-20s where the
Resonance owner has admitted them, exploit rounding and exact boundaries, buy Strategy inventory, and signal and
withdraw atomically. The attacker may not forge signatures, violate EVM atomic rollback, replace immutable
dependencies, or exercise Resonance's owner-only methods without controlling its current owner. The external
governance system intended to own Resonance is unselected and outside this model.

“Rejected” means the cheapest sequence reverts without partial state. “Conserved” means the sequence succeeds but the
asserted custody/liability identities prevent extraction. “Accepted” is an intentional residual, not a safety claim.

These 59 hypotheses are exercised across deterministic regressions, stateful invariants, the no-cheatcode campaign
harness, and independent Python/TypeScript models. A narrow 49-mutant campaign killed every selected policy,
settlement, and Bribe-precision mutant against the ADR 0036/0037 tree, but it predates ADR 0043. The recorded
native-fuzzer counts are also historical; both require current-tree reruns.
Passing these checks narrows known attack surfaces; it does not establish completeness. None of these
hypotheses validates an external governance release, its permission or upgrade
graph, proposal lifecycle, execution policy, or the Resonance ownership handoff; those remain deployment and
independent-review gates.
