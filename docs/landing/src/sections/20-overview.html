How it works

Money in, aimed by holders, out as assets you can claim

Miners pay USDG for mining slots — the only money in. Holders point that stream at assets, falling-price auctions convert it, and burning GBX takes your share out at any time.

  1. 01 · Money in

    Miners pay in

    Sixteen mining slots, every one always for sale. The USDG miners pay is the fund's only buying power.

  2. 02 · Aimed

    Holders aim it

    Revenue releases as a rolling seven-day stream, split moment to moment by where holders point their GBX.

  3. 03 · Converted

    Auctions convert it

    Each Strategy sells its USDG at a falling price — paid in the target asset itself, never in dollars. No oracle anywhere.

  4. 04 · Yours

    The fund holds it

    At least 80% of every purchase, in code. Ownerless — assets leave only when a holder burns GBX for their share.
