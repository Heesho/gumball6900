How assets arrive

Each Strategy pools the USDG the stream has sent it and sells the whole lot in one falling-price auction — asking to be paid in the asset itself, never in dollars. The lot keeps growing while the ask falls; a trader fills the moment the ask drops to what the lot is worth. That is the entire price discovery. The asset splits as it lands: at least 80% to the treasury, and the signalers’ share — 10% by default, never more than 20% — to the holders who aimed it.
